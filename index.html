The Challenge ಸವಾಲು

Strong farmers.
Weak markets.

ಪ್ರಬಲ ರೈತರು. ದುರ್ಬಲ ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ.

FPOs across Karnataka have skilled farmers and good produce — but most are selling raw material at rock-bottom prices while the real value is captured by traders and intermediaries downstream.

ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ FPO ಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಪುಣ ರೈತರು ಮತ್ತು ಉತ್ತಮ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನ ಇದೆ — ಆದರೆ ಹೆಚ್ಚಿನವರು ಕಚ್ಚಾ ಸಾಮಗ್ರಿಯನ್ನು ಕಡಿಮೆ ಬೆಲೆಗೆ ಮಾರುತ್ತಿದ್ದಾರೆ.

The missing piece is not land or labour. It is design thinking, market access, and enterprise support — the things this accelerator directly provides.

ಕೊರತೆ ಇರುವುದು ಜಮೀನಿನಲ್ಲಲ್ಲ ಅಥವಾ ಶ್ರಮದಲ್ಲಲ್ಲ. ಇರುವುದು ವಿನ್ಯಾಸ ಚಿಂತನೆ, ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ ಪ್ರವೇಶ ಮತ್ತು ಉದ್ಯಮ ಬೆಂಬಲ.

At the same time, many FPO members — women, youth, small farmers, agri-labourers — are not getting the full benefit of being part of a collective. This programme puts them at the centre.

ಅನೇಕ FPO ಸದಸ್ಯರು — ಮಹಿಳೆಯರು, ಯುವಕರು, ಸಣ್ಣ ರೈತರು, ಕೃಷಿ ಕಾರ್ಮಿಕರು — ಸಾಮೂಹಿಕ ಸಂಸ್ಥೆಯ ಪೂರ್ಣ ಪ್ರಯೋಜನ ಪಡೆಯುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ. ಈ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮ ಅವರನ್ನೇ ಕೇಂದ್ರ ಬಿಂದುವಾಗಿ ಇಡುತ್ತದೆ.

Common Goods — Examples
ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಸರಕು — ಉದಾಹರಣೆಗಳು
Things your FPO can do for every member
ನಿಮ್ಮ FPO ಪ್ರತಿ ಸದಸ್ಯನಿಗೆ ಮಾಡಬಹುದಾದ ಕೆಲಸ
+
🌾 Collective Input Procurementಸಾಮೂಹಿಕ ಒಳಹರಿವು ಖರೀದಿ

Buying seeds, fertilisers, and pesticides together in bulk — reducing the cost for every individual farmer through the power of collective demand.

🔬 Shared Soil Testingಮಣ್ಣು ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಸೇವೆ

Organising group soil tests for every farm — and turning results into practical advice on what to grow and how to improve soil health together.

🚜 Equipment Sharing Poolಉಪಕರಣ ಹಂಚಿಕೆ

Pooling a thresher, sprayer, or small tractor that members take turns using — making machinery accessible to farms that could never afford it alone.

🏭 Common Storage or Drying Yardಶೇಖರಣೆ / ಒಣಗಿಸುವ ಜಾಗ

A shared space to store, dry, or grade produce — so members are not forced to sell immediately after harvest when prices are lowest.

📦 Group Branding & Packagingಸಾಮೂಹಿಕ ಬ್ರ್ಯಾಂಡ್

A shared label and packaging that all members use — making their produce visible as a quality product rather than an anonymous commodity.

🛒 Joint Market Accessಜಂಟಿ ಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ ಪ್ರವೇಶ

Aggregating produce from all members to approach institutional buyers, processors, or export agents — with more bargaining power than any one farmer.

Vulnerable Members — Activity Ideas
ದುರ್ಬಲ ಸದಸ್ಯರು — ಚಟುವಟಿಕೆ ಆಲೋಚನೆಗಳು
Activities for farmers with limited land
ಕಡಿಮೆ ಜಮೀನಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಮಾಡಬಹುದಾದ ಚಟುವಟಿಕೆ
+
🍄 Mushroom Cultivationಅಣಬೆ ಬೇಸಾಯ

Growing oyster or button mushrooms using low-cost substrate — something that can be done in a small shed or home space, with steady local demand.

🥬 Kitchen Garden & Homestead Farmingಮನೆ ತೋಟ

Vegetables, greens, and herbs grown on land close to the house — diversifying what a small-holding farmer grows and uses.

🐝 Beekeepingಜೇನು ಸಾಕಣೆ

A few hives placed near crops — works without taking land from the main crop, improves pollination, and produces honey that can be sold locally.

🌿 Medicinal & Aromatic Plantsಔಷಧೀಯ ಸಸ್ಯ

Aloe vera, tulsi, lemongrass, or ashwagandha — low maintenance crops that grow on poor land and have growing demand in herbal markets.

🐓 Backyard Poultry or Goat Rearingಕೋಳಿ / ಮೇಕೆ ಸಾಕಣೆ

Small livestock that uses farm waste as feed and requires minimal land — a low-risk way to diversify beyond the main crop season.

🌱 Vermicompostingಎರೆಹುಳು ಕಾಂಪೋಸ್ಟ್

Converting farm and kitchen waste into compost — reduces spending on inputs and produces material that improves any soil it goes into.

Women — Enterprise Ideas
ಮಹಿಳೆಯರು — ಉದ್ಯಮ ಆಲೋಚನೆಗಳು
Women-led small-scale enterprises within the FPO
FPO ಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಹಿಳಾ ನೇತೃತ್ವದ ಸಣ್ಣ ಉದ್ಯಮ
+
🍶 Value-Added Food Productsಮೌಲ್ಯ ವರ್ಧಿತ ಆಹಾರ

Pickles, dried vegetables, spice mixes, jams — made from the FPO's own produce. Women control the process, own the product, and sell directly.

🌿 Spice & Herb Processingಮಸಾಲೆ ಸಂಸ್ಕರಣೆ

Drying, cleaning, grading, and packing turmeric, chilli, or coriander grown by FPO members — turning raw crop into a finished product with better value.

🌸 Floriculture & Nurseryಹೂ ಕೃಷಿ ಮತ್ತು ನರ್ಸರಿ

Growing flowers, saplings, or ornamental plants — often on homestead land — with consistent local demand from events, temples, and markets.

🍄 Mushroom Bag Preparationಅಣಬೆ ಬ್ಯಾಗ್ ತಯಾರಿ

Preparing spawn bags and substrate at home — a cottage-level activity that supports mushroom growers in the FPO and creates a reliable supply chain.

🥛 Dairy & Fermented Productsಹಾಲಿನ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನ

Curd, buttermilk, ghee — simple products from household animals that women can make, package, and sell with consistent year-round demand.

🧺 Agri-Craft & Natural Productsಕೃಷಿ ಕರಕುಶಲ

Baskets, leaf plates, or packaging from natural materials — skills many women already have, applied to a product that the FPO's own market network can sell.

Young members as enterprise builders

+
🚁 Drone & Precision Servicesಡ್ರೋನ್ ಸೇವೆ

Operating drones for crop monitoring or spraying — a technical service with growing demand that young members can offer to farms within and outside the FPO.

📱 Farm Record Keepingಕೃಷಿ ದಾಖಲೆ

Tracking costs, harvests, and sales for FPO members digitally — a skill-based service that creates useful data for every farmer and the FPO as a whole.

🎥 FPO Content & Marketingಮಾರುಕಟ್ಟೆ ವಿಷಯ

Filming, photographing, and managing the FPO's presence online — connecting the produce to buyers and telling the story of who grew it.

🔧 Equipment Maintenance Serviceಉಪಕರಣ ದುರಸ್ತಿ

Servicing sprayers, pumps, and small farm equipment — a repair and maintenance service that every farming cluster needs and few can currently access.

🌱 New Crop Trialsಹೊಸ ಬೆಳೆ ಪ್ರಯೋಗ

Testing climate-resilient varieties, new species, or alternative crops — documenting what works and sharing results with all FPO members.

🛒 Aggregation & Sales Coordinationಮಾರಾಟ ಸಂಯೋಜನೆ

Coordinating bulk sales, contacting buyers, managing logistics — work that needs energy and digital access, both of which young members bring.

Agri Labourers — Examples
ಕೃಷಿ ಕಾರ್ಮಿಕರು — ಉದಾಹರಣೆಗಳು
Landless workers as stakeholders
ಭೂರಹಿತ ಕಾರ್ಮಿಕರಿಗೆ ಪಾಲು
+
🎓 Skill Certificatesಕೌಶಲ್ಯ ಪ್ರಮಾಣಪತ್ರ

Enrol labourers in government skill programs for grafting, pruning, and organic farming. A certificate means higher daily wages.

📲 On-Time Digital Payಸಮಯಕ್ಕೆ ಡಿಜಿಟಲ್ ವೇತನ

FPO ensures wages are paid on time via UPI — ending payment delays and the disputes that harm relationships between farmers and workers.

📅 Work Calendarಕೆಲಸ ಕ್ಯಾಲೆಂಡರ್

FPO publishes a seasonal demand calendar so labourers know when work is available nearby — reducing the pressure to migrate.

🤝 FPO Membership PathFPO ಸದಸ್ಯತ್ವ ಹಾದಿ

Landless labourers can become associate members of the FPO — getting access to insurance, credit, and skill programmes.

Champion Farmers — Role Ideas
ಚಾಂಪಿಯನ್ ರೈತರು — ಪಾತ್ರ ಆಲೋಚನೆಗಳು
Experienced farmers who lead and share knowledge
ಅನುಭವಿ ರೈತರು ಮುಂದಾಳತ್ವ ವಹಿಸಲಿ
+
🌱 Demonstration Plotsಪ್ರಾತ್ಯಕ್ಷಿಕ ಜಮೀನು

Running a small area of their own farm as a live example of a new variety or technique — visible, honest proof that other farmers can see and follow.

🗣 Peer Learning Gatheringsಸ್ನೇಹಿ ಕಲಿಕೆ ಸಭೆ

Hosting small informal sessions at their farm — showing what changed, what failed, and why. Learning through story and practice, not formal training.

🌾 Climate Variety Trialsಹವಾಮಾನ ತಳಿ ಪ್ರಯೋಗ

Testing new seed varieties suited for erratic rainfall or rising temperatures — and patiently recording what actually happens over a full season.

With deep roots in Karnataka's farming communities, SELCO brings mentorship, technology expertise, and enterprise development to this programme.ಸೆಲ್ಕೋ ಫೌಂಡೇಶನ್ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ರೈತ ಸಮುದಾಯಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಸುಸ್ಥಿರ ಶಕ್ತಿ ಮತ್ತು ಹವಾಮಾನ ಉದ್ಯಮ ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ ಮಾಡುತ್ತದೆ.

ಸೆಲ್ಕೋ ಫೌಂಡೇಶನ್ ಸಮಾಜದ ಅಂಚಿನ ಸಮುದಾಯಗಳನ್ನು ಸಮರ್ಥಗೊಳಿಸಲು ಸುಸ್ಥಿರ ಶಕ್ತಿ ಮತ್ತು ಹವಾಮಾನ-ಸ್ಮಾರ್ಟ್ ಉದ್ಯಮ ಪರಿಹಾರಗಳ ಮೂಲಕ ಕಾರ್ಯ ನಿರ್ವಹಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.

The Centre of Excellence for Farmer Producer Organisations (COE FPO) supports, trains, and strengthens FPOs across Karnataka — providing institutional backing, farmer network access, and policy linkages for FPOs to grow as enterprises.COE FPO ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ಉದ್ಯಮಗಳಾಗಿ ಬೆಳೆಯಲು FPO ಗಳಿಗೆ ಸಂಸ್ಥಾ ಬೆಂಬಲ, ರೈತ ಜಾಲ ಮತ್ತು ನೀತಿ ಸಂಪರ್ಕ ನೀಡುತ್ತದೆ.
